SECON 2026

FastSET: Fast Sequential Retraining to Accelerate Real-Time Online Adaptation for Neural Receivers

Facheng Hu, Yunzhe Li, Hongzi Zhu, Xudong Wang

2026 22nd Annual IEEE International Conference on Sensing, Communication, and Networking (SECON), pp. 150–158, 2026.

Abstract摘要

Neural receivers for wireless communication must adapt rapidly to time-varying channels to ensure reliable performance. However, frequent online adaptation incurs significant latency and computational overhead, limiting real-time deployment. Inspired by the modular design of conventional receivers, we propose FastSET, a fast sequential retraining strategy that decomposes the neural receiver into a neural estimator and a neural demapper, accelerating adaptation via a decoupled twostage sequential update. An offline model search identifies an efficient estimator-demapper split that balances performance and complexity. During online operation, FastSET first updates the estimator with a few gradient steps, then refines the demapper while keeping the estimator frozen, mitigating the inherent moving-target problem and reducing total adaptation time. We evaluate FastSET on a frequency-domain OFDM receiver under 3GPP TDL channel models and ray-traced urban scenarios. Experimental results demonstrate that FastSET achieves 5.1–5.9 × faster adaptation than end-to-end retraining, while limiting BER degradation to within 3% compared to the baseline across diverse dynamic environments.用于无线通信的神经接收机必须快速适应时变信道，以确保可靠的性能。然而，频繁的在线适应会产生显著的延迟和计算开销，限制实时部署。受传统接收机模块化设计的启发，我们提出 FastSET，一种快速顺序重训练策略，将神经接收机分解为神经估计器和神经解映射器，通过解耦的两阶段顺序更新加速适应。离线模型搜索确定一种兼顾性能与复杂度的高效估计器与解映射器划分方式。在线运行时，FastSET 首先通过少量梯度更新步骤更新估计器，然后在保持估计器冻结的同时进一步训练解映射器，从而缓解固有的移动目标问题，并缩短总适应时间。我们在 3GPP 抽头延迟线（TDL）信道模型和射线追踪的城市场景下，使用频域正交频分复用（OFDM）接收机评估 FastSET。实验结果表明，在多种动态环境中，FastSET 的适应速度达到端到端重训练的 5.1–5.9 倍，同时将相对于基线的误码率（BER）性能退化控制在 3% 以内。

INFOCOM 2025

Prism: Mining Task-aware Domains in Non-i.i.d. IMU Data for Flexible User Perception

Yunzhe Li*, Facheng Hu*, Hongzi Zhu, Quan Liu, Xiaoke Zhao, Jiangang Shen, Shan Chang, Minyi Guo

IEEE Conference on Computer Communications (INFOCOM), pp. 1–10, 2025.

Abstract摘要

A wide range of user perception applications leverage inertial measurement unit (IMU) data for online prediction. However, restricted by the non-i.i.d. nature of IMU data collected from mobile devices, most systems work well only in a controlled setting (e.g., for a specific user in particular postures), limiting application scenarios. To achieve uncontrolled online prediction on mobile devices, referred to as the flexible user perception (FUP) problem, is attractive but hard. In this paper, we propose a novel scheme, called Prism, which can obtain high FUP accuracy on mobile devices. The core of Prism is to discover task-aware domains embedded in IMU dataset, and to train a domain-aware model on each identified domain. To this end, we design an expectation-maximization (EM) algorithm to estimate latent domains with respect to the specific downstream perception task. Finally, the best-fit model can be automatically selected for use by comparing the test sample and all identified domains in the feature space. We implement Prism on various mobile devices and conduct extensive experiments. Results demonstrate that Prism can achieve the best FUP performance with a low latency.众多用户感知应用利用惯性测量单元（IMU）数据进行在线预测。然而，受移动设备所采集 IMU 数据的非独立同分布特性限制，大多数系统仅在受控条件下（例如特定用户采用特定姿态时）表现良好，这限制了应用场景。在移动设备上实现不受控条件下的在线预测，即灵活用户感知（FUP）问题，具有吸引力，但也很困难。本文提出了一种名为 Prism 的新方案，能够在移动设备上获得较高的 FUP 准确率。Prism 的核心是发现 IMU 数据集中蕴含的任务感知域，并在每个识别出的域上训练一个域感知模型。为此，我们设计了一种期望最大化（EM）算法，针对具体的下游感知任务估计潜在域。最后，通过在特征空间中比较测试样本与所有识别出的域，可以自动选择最合适的模型供使用。我们在多种移动设备上实现了 Prism，并进行了广泛实验。结果表明，Prism 能够以较低的延迟实现最佳 FUP 性能。

ICDCS 2025

Saga: Capturing Multi-granularity Semantics from Massive Unlabelled IMU Data

Yunzhe Li*, Facheng Hu*, Hongzi Zhu, Shifan Zhang, Liang Zhang, Shan Chang, Minyi Guo

IEEE International Conference on Distributed Computing Systems (ICDCS), 2025.

Abstract摘要

Inertial measurement units (IMUs), have been prevalently used in a wide range of mobile perception applications such as activity recognition and user authentication, where a large amount of labelled data are normally required to train a satisfactory model. However, it is difficult to label micro-activities in massive IMU data due to the hardness of understanding raw IMU data and the lack of ground truth. In this paper, we propose a novel fine-grained user perception approach, called Saga, which only needs a small amount of labelled IMU data to achieve stunning user perception accuracy. The core idea of Saga is to first pre-train a backbone feature extraction model, utilizing the rich semantic information of different levels embedded in the massive unlabelled IMU data. Meanwhile, for a specific downstream user perception application, Bayesian Optimization is employed to determine the optimal weights for pre-training tasks involving different semantic levels. We implement Saga on five typical mobile phones and evaluate Saga on three typical tasks on three IMU datasets. Results show that when only using about 100 training samples per class, Saga can achieve over 90% accuracy of the full-fledged model trained on over ten thousands training samples with no additional system overhead.惯性测量单元（IMU）已广泛用于活动识别、用户身份认证等各类移动感知应用，这些应用通常需要大量有标签数据才能训练出令人满意的模型。然而，由于原始 IMU 数据难以理解且缺乏真实标注，很难为海量 IMU 数据中的微活动添加标签。本文提出了一种名为 Saga 的新型细粒度用户感知方法，只需少量有标签 IMU 数据即可实现出色的用户感知准确率。Saga 的核心思想是利用海量无标签 IMU 数据中蕴含的不同层级的丰富语义信息，首先预训练一个骨干特征提取模型。同时，针对具体的下游用户感知应用，采用贝叶斯优化来确定涉及不同语义层级的预训练任务的最优权重。我们在五款典型手机上实现了 Saga，并在三个 IMU 数据集上的三项典型任务中对其进行评估。结果表明，每类仅使用约 100 个训练样本时，Saga 就能够达到使用一万多个训练样本训练的完整模型准确率的 90% 以上，且不增加系统开销。

TVT 2024

DeepAoA+: Online Cross-Domain Vehicular Relative Direction Estimation via Deep Learning

Facheng Hu, Yunxiang Cai, Hongzi Zhu, Shan Chang, Xudong Wang, Minyi Guo

IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology, 73(12), pp. 19216–19228, 2024.

Abstract摘要

Relative direction estimation among neighboring vehicles in urban environments is essential to a wide variety of driving safety applications. To obtain accurate direction information solely from vehicle-to-vehicle (V2V) communications is desirable but very challenging due to enormous multipath effects. In this paper, we propose an online cross-domain vehicular relative direction estimation method, called DeepAoA+, based on a domain adaptive deep learning method. More specifically, the channel state information (CSI) is estimated from a set of synchronized receiving radios at a receiver vehicle. By taking the CSI phase difference between a pair of such radios, CSI phase errors in the baseband can be effectively eliminated, which makes a compelling feature to represent the direction of incident radio frequency (RF) signals and the dynamic channel characteristics. Moreover, to conquer the non-i.i.d(independent and identically distributed) problem caused by dynamic environments, DeepAoA+ discovers latent domains in the CSI phase difference dataset. For each identified domain, a domain-specific model is trained to estimate the Angle-of-Arrival (AoA) of incoming signals. To this end, a framework based on an expectation-maximization (EM) algorithm is proposed to obtain domain partitions considering downstream AoA estimation tasks. Furthermore, a pre-training technique is used to enhance the robustness of AoA estimation with misclassified test samples. We implement a prototype of DeepAoA+, using four synchronized Universal Software Radio Peripherals (USRPs) from various scenarios, and conduct extensive real-world experiments. The results demonstrate that DeepAoA+ can achieve the best AoA estimation with low latency.城市环境中相邻车辆之间的相对方向估计对于各类驾驶安全应用至关重要。仅通过车与车（V2V）通信获取准确的方向信息很有价值，但由于严重的多径效应，这一任务极具挑战性。本文提出了一种基于域自适应深度学习方法的在线跨域车辆相对方向估计方法，称为 DeepAoA+。具体而言，在接收车辆上通过一组同步接收无线电设备估计信道状态信息（CSI）。通过计算其中一对无线电设备之间的 CSI 相位差，可以有效消除基带中的 CSI 相位误差，从而形成一种有效特征，用于表示入射射频（RF）信号的方向和动态信道特性。此外，为克服动态环境导致的非独立同分布问题，DeepAoA+ 在 CSI 相位差数据集中发现潜在域。针对每个识别出的域，训练一个域专属模型来估计入射信号的到达角（AoA）。为此，提出了一个基于期望最大化（EM）算法的框架，在考虑下游 AoA 估计任务的情况下获得域划分。此外，采用预训练技术来增强 AoA 估计在测试样本被错误分类时的鲁棒性。我们使用四台同步的通用软件无线电外设（USRP），在多种场景下实现了 DeepAoA+ 原型，并进行了广泛的真实环境实验。结果表明，DeepAoA+ 能够以较低的延迟实现最佳 AoA 估计。

Facheng’s Research Journey发成的科研探索之旅

A record of my research explorations: gradually developing my own research habits and criteria for judgment, learning how to evaluate a piece of work, prepare for group meetings, identify research questions, and design experiments, while also keeping ideas that are still taking shape and papers worth revisiting. This is not a finished methodology, but a collection of research notes that I continue to write, revise, and build upon.记录我的科研探索：逐步形成自己的科研习惯与判断标准，学习如何评价一项工作、准备组会、寻找问题和设计实验，也保存尚未成熟的想法与值得反复阅读的论文。这不是一份完成的方法论，而是一份由我持续书写、修正和积累的研究笔记。
